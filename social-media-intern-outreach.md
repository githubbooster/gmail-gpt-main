## 2. Things the schools require (flags)

**Pay is hourly, which clears the main hurdle.**
- The role pays an hourly wage above minimum, so neither school's commission-only or unpaid internship rules apply.
- **On your end:** this would be your first employee. Pay for every hour actually worked (have the intern log hours), pay on a regular payday, and treat them as a W-2 employee (payroll taxes, and workers' comp in Kentucky). Confirm the setup yourself or use a payroll service.
- **Client accounts:** the intern makes content for your clients, so decide up front who approves posts before they go live. The listing says you review everything.

**UofL**
- Employer account needs a physical address (no PO box), a working website, and a contact email that matches the website domain. sam@digitaladxpert.com works. A Gmail address wouldn't.
- New employer accounts are reviewed, usually within two business days. Each posting is reviewed too.
- Postings that need an investment or charge the student a fee aren't accepted.
- Unpaid internships have to meet the US Department of Labor primary beneficiary test.
- For credit: student has to contact Ulmer before starting or within the first two weeks. Pass/fail, based on reports, your supervisor evaluation and minimum hours. Older pages say 75 work hours per credit hour (225 for 3 credits), which may be outdated. At 10 to 15 hrs/week over a semester that's roughly 150 to 225 hours, so it could be tight.

**Bellarmine**
- Handshake account has to be approved before you can post.
- They follow NACE standards and can remove employers who don't.
- Unpaid postings from for-profit employers that don't meet the Department of Labor test won't be posted.
- Any upfront costs to the student have to be stated in the listing.
- For credit: about 90 hours in a semester for 3 credits, registered through Handshake, approved by a faculty sponsor, chair and dean. You'd fill out a career readiness evaluation at the end of the term.

**Couldn't find**
- UofL: any dated event through Feb 22, 2027, a current Ulmer Director.
- Bellarmine: Handshake sign-up URL, career fair registration link or fee, the current general phone number, a Rubel-specific coordinator.
